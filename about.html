About this project

Why Trailhead

Trailhead is a field guide to the U.S. National Park System. It pulls live park data from the National Park Service and pairs it with a curated set of field notes — an opinionated shortlist for anyone planning their first serious parks trip.

How it's built

The site is hand-written: semantic HTML5, CSS3 with Flexbox and a little Bootstrap 5 for the grid, and vanilla JavaScript organised into ES6 classes — no jQuery. The Explore page fetches from the NPS Data API and pages through the results; the Field Notes are a separate curated dataset.

About the data

Live park information comes from the NPS Data API, the official source maintained by the U.S. National Park Service. The field notes are personal and were written for this project.

Who made it

Built by Peter El Gemayel as the final project for Full Stack Development, Lebanese University — Faculty of Engineering, 2026.
